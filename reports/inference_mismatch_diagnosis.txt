VERDICT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The generator INPUT (z_e, z_n), the atom/bond encoder-decoder dicts,
the graph dimensions (m_dim=14, b_dim=5, V=45), eval mode, and the
deterministic argmax sampling are IDENTICAL between the two paths.
The production decoder is STRICTLY MORE LENIENT than the training
decoder (strict=False + sanitize + largest-fragment), so decoding
cannot explain production being MORE invalid.

The ONLY substantive contract difference is the CONDITION VECTOR:
the Issue-5 pilot generator was fine-tuned EXCLUSIVELY with the zero
condition [0,0,0] (Option A), but generate_joint_molecules.py feeds
the non-zero condition [1.0, ~1.0, ~0.1036]. Because the condition is
injected twice in ConditionalGenerator (before the Transformer and
before the categorical readout), this out-of-distribution condition
shifts the node/edge logits away from the valid-graph manifold.


CAUSE_IDENTIFIED: yes
ROOT_CAUSE: The Issue-5 pilot ConditionalGenerator was fine-tuned exclusively with the all-zeros condition vector [0,0,0] (train_joint_positive_disc.py Option A, lambda_condition=0.0), but generate_joint_molecules.py feeds it the non-zero production condition [1.0, normalized_pIC50, normalized_logBB] (=[1.0, ~1.0, ~0.1036] for the defaults pIC50=8.0/logBB=0.0). Every other generator-input contract is identical between the two paths (z_e and z_n are ChEMBL one-hot graphs, m_dim=14/b_dim=5/V=45, eval mode, deterministic argmax, identical atom/bond decoder dicts), and the production decoder is actually more lenient than the training decoder. Because ConditionalGenerator.forward() injects the condition at TWO points — once before the Transformer via condition_layers and again before the categorical readout via readout_condition — the out-of-distribution non-zero condition displaces the node/edge logits off the valid-graph manifold that the zero-condition fine-tuning had stabilized, yielding near-zero validity at inference while the in-distribution zero-condition eval during training stayed 90-100% through step 300.
FIX: In the generation/inference path, feed the generator the SAME condition the checkpoint was trained with: for the Issue-5 pilot checkpoint (trained under Option A with lambda_condition=0.0) pass the all-zeros condition torch.zeros(batch_size, 3) instead of the non-zero make_condition() vector. Concretely, make the condition vector in generate_joint_molecules.py configurable (e.g. a --condition_mode {zero,conditional} flag, or an explicit --condition vector) and default it to zeros for checkpoints that were fine-tuned under Option A; verify the checkpoint provenance in run_config.json before choosing. No change to the model architecture, the decoder, or the training script is required, and the baseline/collapsed checkpoints must remain untouched.
